Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Success Hunters Autopost is an internal tool used only by Success Hunters (successhuntersprints.com) to manage its own Instagram account, @successhunters.prints. It is not offered to other users or businesses.

What data the app uses

  • Content of our own Instagram account: posts, captions and like counts.
  • Comments left by people on our own posts: comment text, comment ID and the commenter's username, used only to reply to the comment.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish our posts and to reply to comments on our posts. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to build profiles of people.

Storage and retention

Comment text is processed when a reply is generated and is not stored. Only comment IDs that have already been answered are kept, to avoid replying twice, and they are deleted after 90 days.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to you, send a direct message to @successhunters.prints on Instagram or contact us through successhuntersprints.com. We delete it within 30 days. See also Data deletion instructions.
